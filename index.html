01 / Audience + Problem

She inherited a documentation system she's afraid to touch.

Sarah is a practice manager at a 4–10 clinician allied health clinic. She didn't build the templates — she inherited them. The person who built them left. Now she's responsible for a system she can't safely change, can't fully see, and can't hand off.

01

The Silent Fork

One clinician edits a template without telling anyone. Three months later Sarah finds two versions of the intake form in use during a billing review.

02

The Hostage Template

One template has been live so long it's load-bearing. It has a typo. It's been there since 2022. Nobody touches it.

03

The Rebuild Tax

Medicare changes an item number. Sarah needs to update five templates. Each one is a full rebuild. She sets aside a half-day. It takes a full day.

I know there's a typo in the referral template. It's been there since 2022. I'm not touching it because I don't know what else I'll break.
02 / Positioning

Now you can fix the template everyone's been working around for two years.

Every edit is saved automatically. Full history. One click to restore. Nothing goes live until you say so.

Beta proof point

During beta, one practice manager restored a template that had been incorrectly edited six weeks earlier — a change causing discharge summaries to drop the GP's contact details. She found it in 40 seconds.

05 / Channel Plan

Three moments. No wasted budget.

DAY 0Launch to people who can act
⌄
  • Email to clinic accounts (2+ seats). Sent from a named person. A/B test two subject lines.
  • In-app modal fires for template owners and admins only.
  • No blog post. No press release. Clinics don't read SaaS blogs.
DAYS 3–5Trigger on behavior, not a calendar
⌄
  • Intercom nudge — behaviorally triggered only for users who opened a template but didn't click History or Refine.
  • CS calls to top 15 clinic accounts. Personal message, not a campaign.
WEEK 2Turn signal into the next story
⌄
  • Follow-up email to non-openers. Different subject line only: “One of your clinicians may have edited a template without you knowing.”
  • Pull cohort of version-restore users. Get on the phone with two of them. Get a real quote. That quote is the next email.
